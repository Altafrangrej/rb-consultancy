What We Do for TPA / Insurer Claim Processing Teams:

When an insurance company or TPA receives a cashless authorization or reimbursement claim, our field desk conducts direct physical verification and document forensics to confirm genuine treatment, check proxy admissions, and flag inflated medical/surgical invoicing.

Core Ground Investigation & Forensic Audit Pillars (Standard 24–48h TAT)
Pillar 01

Bed-Bound Patient Validity

Physical hospital visit during admission/treatment window to verify patient presence in bed, matching nurse duty charts, ICU logs, and doctor progressive notes.

Pillar 02

Doctor Statements

Recording verified statements from treating physicians regarding pre-existing disease history, emergency nature of trauma/diagnosis, and clinical correlation.

Pillar 03

Bill Forensic Check

Cross-matching billed surgical consumables, high-cost implants, and pharmacy invoice serials against hospital store stock records and wholesale rates.

Pillar 04

Proxy Interception

Detecting identity substitution, day-care turned inpatient inflation, and duplicate/recycled diagnostic test reports submitted across multiple policies.

Required Documents Checklist आवश्यक कागदपत्रांची यादी

SECTION 1: ADMINISTRATIVE & IDENTITY
  • 1. Cover Letter: Signed summary and list of enclosures.
  • 2. Claim Form (Part A & B): Signed by Primary Policyholder and Hospital.

  • 4. KYC – Patient: Government ID photocopy.
  • 5. KYC – Primary Policyholder: Government ID photocopy.
  • 6. Bank Mandate Proof: Original Cancelled Cheque OR Bank Passbook Xerox copy.
SECTION 2: HOSPITAL FORMALITIES
  • 7. Hospital Registration Certificate: Photocopy of license.
  • 8. Hospital Declaration Form: Signed and stamped.

SECTION 3: PAYMENT PAID RECORDS
  • 10. Hospital Final Bill: Original master itemized bill.
  • 11. Hospital Payment Receipts: Original receipts with "Paid" stamps.
  • 12. Medical Bills with Statement: Itemized pharmacy bills.
  • 13. Lab Bills: Itemized bills for diagnostic services.
  • 14. Other Diagnostic Bills and Receipts: Scans, X-rays, or external diagnostic charges.
  • 15. Pre-Hospitalization Bills and Receipts: Original bills for consultation/tests prior to admission.
SECTION 4: CLINICAL & MEDICAL RECORDS
  • 16. Initial Assessment Report: Admission notes.
  • 17. Indoor Case Papers (ICP): Nursing, Treatment Sheet and daily charts.
  • 18. Medicine Prescription: Doctor's written advice for pharmacy.
  • 19. Lab Prescription: Doctor's advice for tests/investigations.
  • 20. Clinical Investigation Reports: Original Radiology/Pathology results.
  • 21. Pre-Hospitalization Documents & Reports: Reports and prescriptions from pre-admission period.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) वारंवार विचारले जाणारे प्रश्न

Click any question below to read detailed answers. प्रश्न वाचण्यासाठी त्यावर क्लिक करा.

What is the role of RB Consultancy in my insurance claim?
RB Consultancy acts as an independent professional facilitator and authorized agent. We organize your documents, draft notices, and manage communications with insurance companies, TPAs, and statutory bodies on your behalf.
Are you an insurance company or a TPA?
No. We are an independent consultancy firm led by Founder Altaf Rangrej. We are not an insurance carrier or Third-Party Administrator; we represent you (the policyholder) independently.
What is a "First Gate Audit" and is it really free?
The First Gate Audit is our initial review of your claim files, rejection letters, and policy papers. Yes, it is 100% complimentary free for all prospective clients.
How do your success fees and service charges work?
We charge transparent initial fees (such as ₹3,000 for fresh claims or rejections) plus nominal success fees (10%) upon successful claim recovery, as outlined in our transparent fee structure.
Do I need to travel to your office in Jalgaon to get my claim processed?
Not at all! We operate entirely online and remotely. You can submit all documents, forms, and queries digitally via WhatsApp or email from anywhere in Maharashtra or India.
How long does a typical claim recovery or reconsideration process take?
Timelines depend entirely on the respective insurance company, TPA, or statutory authority (such as the Insurance Ombudsman). As facilitators, we push files aggressively but do not guarantee strict completion deadlines.
What documents do I need to provide to start a case?
Depending on your case type, you generally need your policy document, hospital discharge summary, final bills, rejection letters, and KYC details. Check our document checklist section for exact details.
Can you help with health, life, and corporate/commercial claims?
Yes. Our specialized services cover health claims, life insurance disputes, cashless authorizations, rejected claim recoveries, and MSME commercial payment disputes.
What is an MSME Samadhan filing and who is eligible?
MSME Samadhan is a statutory recovery process under the MSMED Act 2006 for registered micro and small enterprises facing delayed payments from buyers or corporate clients.
How do I initiate my case inquiry with RB Consultancy?
You can simply fill out our online General Inquiry & Registration Portal on this website or click the WhatsApp chat button to connect instantly with our desk.
